Common questions.

How are mentors vetted?

We manually review every application. We look for verifiable audience size, engagement metrics, revenue figures (for business mentors), and a track record of clearly articulating concepts.

What happens after I book?

You'll fill out a specific questionnaire so the mentor can prepare. You will receive a calendar invite with a video link. After the call, you'll get a recording and any notes taken during the session.

Can I get a refund if the session wasn't helpful?

Yes. If the mentor doesn't show up or fails to provide the value promised in their profile, we offer a full guarantee. We hold funds in escrow until the session is successfully completed.
